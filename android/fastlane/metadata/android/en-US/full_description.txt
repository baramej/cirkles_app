This app is a community-driven communication and discovery platform designed especially for people on the road. Built as a fork of FluffyChat and enhanced with powerful, location-aware features, it helps drivers connect, communicate, and explore — all in one place.

🚗 Cirkles – Connect on the Road

Cirkles lets you connect with people driving on the same road or nearby areas. Whether you want to give a heads-up about traffic, start a friendly conversation, or simply feel less alone on long drives, Cirkles creates a real-time local driving community around you.

⭐ Rate Other Drivers

See nearby drivers on a live map and share feedback based on your experience. This feature promotes safer and more responsible driving by encouraging positive behavior and accountability within the community.

📻 Moments – Live Radio, Anytime

Tune in to live radio stations while you drive. Moments brings entertainment, news, and music together so your journey is never boring — whether you’re commuting or exploring new routes.

🗺️ Routes – Discover Scenic Drives in Oman

Explore curated scenic routes across Oman. Find beautiful drives, hidden gems, and recommended roads for weekend trips or long adventures. Perfect for road-trip lovers and explorers.

💬 Chat with Friends

Stay connected with your friends through secure, real-time messaging. Built on a reliable chat foundation, the app ensures smooth conversations whether you’re on the move or parked.
